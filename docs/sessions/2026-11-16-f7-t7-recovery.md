### Technical Details
- `RecordingCheckpointStore` (port) → `FileCheckpointStore` (one JSON file, atomic). `Night` stays free of `Codable`: the adapter has its own mirrors.
- Written by `RecordingSession`: after each saved event, after each clip compresses (the event then points at its `.m4a`), every 2 minutes of samples, and at stop with the end reason. Kept until the night is saved (`CommitSession` clears it after the save succeeds) or discarded (`DiscardRecording`: clips, then checkpoint).
- At launch `RecoverInterruptedSession` returns a checkpointed night with events; one without is cleared silently, as SleepGuard. `AppCoordinator` opens the flow at its summary.
- Low battery: every 30 s while recording, ≤ 2% stops the night as `.lowBattery` (its checkpoint and summary carry it).
- The dashboard's feature counts were stale (Done 2); now 7 done, 1 in progress (F0), 2 backlog.
